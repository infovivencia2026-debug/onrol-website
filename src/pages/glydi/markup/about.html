ONROL Overview

ONROL is India’s AI Execution School, based in Hyderabad. We are a premium, project based AI school with live mentorship, where learners build real AI solutions instead of collecting certificates for concepts they never use.

Across 4 programs in AI and Cyber Security, students work through 20+ real projects, taught live in 3 languages and structured so that every week ends with something finished, deployed, and worth showing.

Our mentors are practitioners who have shipped real work. They sit in every session, review what you build, and stay with you from your first prompt to the moment your project goes live on the web with a link of its own.
