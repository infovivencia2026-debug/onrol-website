FAQ

Questions students ask before joining

The things people usually want cleared up first.

Do I need any coding experience?

None. The program is built for absolute beginners — we start from scratch and there's nothing to know beforehand.

How much time does it take each day?

Just one hour a day across 21 days. It's designed to sit alongside a job or a degree.

Is it online or in person?

Fully online, taught live. You join every session in real time, wherever you are.

When does the next cohort start?

17 August. Seats are limited, so registering early is the safest way in.

What do I actually build?

Automations, chatbots, AI agents, AI-powered websites and apps, plus generated images and video — finishing with a deployed capstone product for your portfolio.

What happens after the 21 days?

You get 45 days of live project support — six more weeks of hands-on help to refine your capstone and start new builds.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, a Certificate of Completion — alongside the deployed product that proves what you can do.

Is there a language requirement?

Basic English proficiency is enough to follow the sessions comfortably.

Who is this program for?

Students, working professionals, freelancers, founders, educators, and anyone who wants to become genuinely AI-ready.
