# ONROL Backlink & Authority Starter Kit

**Why this is now the #1 lever.** The site build is done (2,117 pages, global clusters, AEO
FAQs, clean technical SEO). On a young `.in` domain chasing competitive + global terms, what
decides rankings from here is **authority** — links and brand signals — not more pages. This kit
is what *you* execute off-site; each play says exactly which ONROL page to point at and how.

**Two hard rules (keep them):**
- **Never name competitor institutes** anywhere — compare only by category. (Same rule as our content.)
- **Natural anchors only.** Mostly branded ("ONROL", "ONROL's AI course") and generic ("this
  guide", "here"); occasional partial-match ("live online AI course"). Never repeat an exact-match
  money-keyword anchor across many sites — that reads as manipulation.

|---|---|---|
| **Money** | `/programs`, `/sites/learn-ai-online`, `/sites/online-ai-course`, `/sites/best-online-ai-course`, `/sites/ai-engineer-course` | Convert + rank for head terms |

Rule of thumb: **build links to the authority + magnet pages**, and let the internal mesh pass
that equity to the money pages (that mesh already exists site-wide).

## The plays, ranked by ROI

### 1. Free tools as link magnets (highest ROI, do first)
People link to *useful free things*, not to course pages. You already have three: the **AI Prompt
Library**, the **AI Skills Quiz**, and the **Best AI Tools directory**.
- **Submit them** to free-tool roundups and "best free AI tools" lists (search `"free AI tools"
  + "submit"` / `"add your tool"`), AI tool directories (There's An AI For That, Futurepedia-style
  listings, Toolify, SaaSHub, AlternativeTo), and Product Hunt.
- **Pitch them** to newsletter curators and AI/edtech bloggers as "a free resource your readers can
  use" — link goes to the tool, not a sales page.
- Add a subtle "Made by ONROL" + link on each tool so every share carries a link.

### 2. Founder bylines / guest posts (Dr. Neeraja Reddy)
A real founder byline earns trust + a contextual link. Pitch 800–1,200-word originals to AI,
edtech, careers and Indian-startup publications. **Angles (each links to the mapped page):**
- "Why applied, build-first AI beats theory for career switchers" → `is-learning-ai-worth-it`
- "The AI skills actually being hired for in 2026" → `ai-skills-in-demand-2026`
- "How to become an AI engineer without a CS degree" → `how-to-become-an-ai-engineer`
- "Agents vs chatbots: what businesses really need" → `what-is-an-ai-agent`
- "Learning AI online worldwide: what works, what doesn't" → `learn-ai-online`
Target types (find real outlets in each): Indian startup/tech media, edtech blogs, dev.to /
Hashnode / Medium publications, career and upskilling sites, HR/L&D blogs (for the persona angle).

### 3. Original data / small study (digital PR — best links)
Journalists and bloggers link to *data*. Publish one small original study and pitch it:
- "State of AI upskilling in India 2026" — survey your learners/waitlist (anonymised, honest numbers
  only), or analyse anonymised trends. Turn it into a page + a few charts, then pitch to tech/edu
  press. One good data study can earn dozens of editorial links. (Never fabricate figures.)

### 4. Expert-quote sourcing (HARO-style)
Sign up to journalist-request services (Featured/Terkel/Qwoted/SourceBottle and Indian PR lists).
Answer AI/education/career queries with a short, genuinely useful quote from the founder → earns
citations in articles on high-authority sites. Low effort, steady links.

### 5. Directories & consistent profiles (foundation)
- Education/course directories and Indian business directories (accept genuine listings).
- Keep **NAP + branding consistent** with the site's `sameAs` (LinkedIn/company/onrol,
  Instagram/onrol.in, YouTube/@onrolofficial). Consistency itself is a trust signal.
- Google Business Profile (even for an online school with a registered address).

### 6. Communities & partnerships (relationship links)
- **Colleges / campus programs** — you have `ai-training-for-colleges`; partnerships → .edu-style
  and institutional links + real leads.
- **Communities** (Reddit, Discord, Slack, Quora): participate genuinely and link only where it
  truly helps someone (self-promo spam gets removed and can hurt). Answer AI-learning questions and
  cite the relevant AEO page as a resource.
- **Podcasts / YouTube collabs** — founder guest spots usually include a show-notes link.

## Anchor-text mix (aim roughly)
- ~50% branded ("ONROL", "ONROL AI Execution School")
- ~25% generic / URL ("this guide", "here", "onrol.in")
- ~20% partial-match ("live online AI course", "applied AI program")
- ~5% exact-match — sparingly, and never the same phrase repeatedly

## Tracking
- Tag any link you control that drives clicks with UTMs (the site now persists them to the CRM):
  `?utm_source=<site>&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=backlinks`.
- Watch **GSC → Links** (external links + top linked pages) monthly, and referral traffic in GA4.
- Goal isn't volume — it's **relevant, editorial** links from AI/education/tech/career sites.

## 30-day starter checklist
1. Submit all 3 free tools to 10+ directories/roundups.
2. Draft + pitch 2 founder guest posts (angles above).
3. Register for 2 journalist-request services; answer 3 queries/week.
4. Claim/verify all directory + social profiles (consistent NAP + sameAs).
5. Scope one original data study for month 2.
