## Review-seeding strategy (the part most institutes get wrong)

NareshIT didn't get 10,446 reviews by accident. Here's the realistic ONROL playbook:

### Phase 1 — First 30 days: 5 reviews per platform (30 total)

- **Day 1 of each cohort,** introduce the review platforms to learners verbally: "If you find value in week 1, we'd love an honest review."
- **Day 14 of each cohort,** send a follow-up: short message with the 3 platform links + a clear ask. Most won't act. That's fine.
- **Day 30 (graduation day),** send the strongest ask: "If ONROL helped you, a 30-second review on Justdial / Trustpilot / Sulekha is the best way to help future learners find us." Include direct links pre-filled with the search.
- **DO NOT incentivise reviews** (gift cards, discounts) — that violates every platform's TOS and gets you suspended.
- **DO ask alumni weekly in the community** for organic reviews. Make it part of the alumni habit.

### Phase 2 — Months 2–6: build to 25 reviews per platform

- After every cohort closure, ask the 30–50 graduates for reviews. Conversion rate ~10–20% organic = 3–10 reviews per platform per cohort.
- After 6 cohorts (~6 months), you should hit 25+ per platform.

### Phase 3 — Months 6–12: scale to 100+ per platform

- Build review-request into the certificate-delivery email. "Your certificate is attached — a review on Justdial would mean the world to us. Link below."
- Add a small review CTA on the learning dashboard for active learners who've shipped projects.

### Response policy (do this from day 1)

- **Reply to every review within 48 hours.** Positive: thank by name + reference what they built. Negative: acknowledge, apologise, offer to talk privately, then resolve.
- **Never argue publicly** with a negative reviewer. Even if they're wrong. The audience watching is judging your tone, not the facts.
- **Save the response templates** (below) and have the support team use them with light customisation.

### Response templates

**Positive 5-star:**
> Thank you, [Name]! It's an honour to see [the AI agent you shipped / your portfolio site / your first freelance client] coming from your cohort with us. The ONROL Community is open for life — keep building. — Team ONROL

**4-star with constructive feedback:**
> Thanks, [Name] — and thank you for the specific feedback on [pace / live-session timing / mentor availability]. We're acting on it for the next cohort. Your portfolio at [URL] is impressive. — Team ONROL

**3-star or below:**
> Hi [Name], we hear you on [the specific issue]. That isn't the experience we want any learner to have. Could you write to neeraja@onrol.in directly so we can make this right? — Team ONROL

**Suspicious 1-star (no detail):**
> Hi [Name], we'd like to understand what didn't work. We can't find a learner by this name in our records — could you confirm your cohort and write to neeraja@onrol.in? We respond to every concern personally. — Team ONROL

## Common mistakes to avoid

- ❌ Don't list ONROL on >10 platforms initially. Spread thin = neglected listings = stale reviews. Six is the right number for the first 6 months.
- ❌ Don't write fake reviews from internal team. Every platform detects this within months and suspends listings.
- ❌ Don't ignore negative reviews. Silence reads as guilt.
- ❌ Don't change your NAP between listings. "Onrol" ≠ "ONROL" ≠ "ONROL AI" for Google's local SEO algorithm.
- ❌ Don't use stock photos exclusively. Real cohort + learner photos win every time.
- ❌ Don't list before you have ≥5 real alumni who can review. Empty listings hurt more than no listing.
