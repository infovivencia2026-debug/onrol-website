Why AI Agents? And Why Now?

Foundation models like ChatGPT and Claude have rewritten what's expected of engineers. Hiring teams at top AI companies are no longer looking for textbook ML knowledge they want engineers who can ship real, working AI agents that solve real problems.

This masterclass closes the gap between academic AI and production AI. In 1.5 focused hours (90 minutes), you'll move from "I've used ChatGPT" to "I've built and deployed an agent" the single most valuable skill in the current job market.
