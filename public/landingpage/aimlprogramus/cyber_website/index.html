Safety, conduct & format

Rules first.
Then tools.

This course teaches how attacks work. That is only responsible if the boundaries come first — and are enforced.

Consent is the syllabus

Scope, authorisation and disclosure open the course — before a single technique.

Sanctioned targets only

Purpose-built vulnerable apps, licensed platforms and the learner's own VMs. Never live systems.

Enforced, not just stated

Testing outside the lab removes a student from the cohort. That rule has no grey area.

Defence carries equal weight

A third of the course is hardening, monitoring, response and hunting.

Supervised throughout

An instructor is live in every session, and parents may observe any of them.

Parents ask

Straight
answers.

If something here matters to you and isn't covered, ask it live at the masterclass.

Q1Are you teaching my child to hack?
We teach how attacks work so they can be spotted, blocked and investigated. Law and authorisation come first; practice happens only on deliberately vulnerable targets. Straying outside the lab ends a student's place in the cohort.
Q2Is any of this legal?
Yes — testing with documented permission on systems you own or are authorised to touch is a recognised profession with its own certifications and a large legitimate industry behind it.
Q3Could it get them into trouble?
The danger is the self-taught route: technique without context or supervision. This course supplies the framing, the boundaries and an adult in the room.
Q4Do they need coding first?
No. The opening ten sessions build networking, Linux and Windows from nothing before offensive material begins.
Q5Is 14 too young?
It's the intended starting point. Beginning early leaves years to build on the base before applications or first roles arrive.
Q6Does it help with college?
It produces artefacts most applicants lack — a pentest report, a documented investigation, a capstone they can defend in conversation. We promise credible evidence, not admission.
Q7Can they earn from it?
Not yet, and we don't sell it that way. Bounty platforms generally require eighteen. What we guarantee is capability and a portfolio.
Q8How much work outside class?
Roughly one to two hours of lab practice a week, heavier during the capstone depending on ambition.
