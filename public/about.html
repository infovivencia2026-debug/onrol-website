How ONROL works

Four phases, one outcome.

Every journey here follows the same path, from your first prompt to getting paid for what you build.

Phase 01

Learn

Get comfortable with the AI tools that matter, writing good prompts, working with AI agents, and the workflow that takes you from idea to a working build fast.

Phase 02

Build

Put it to work on real projects, not throwaway demos. You build features, fix what breaks, and make things people actually use.

Phase 03

Launch

Take it live. Your project goes on the web with a real link, and you walk away with something solid for your portfolio.

Phase 04

Earn

Use it to earn, freelance work, your own product, or a job. You learn how to package what you build and get paid for it.
