04 / Common Questions

The details affiliates
ask us most.

Still unsure? Write to info@onrol.in and the team will help you.

Go to Affiliate Login
How much commission do I earn?
The current standard is 15% on eligible enrolments. Open a program card in your portal before promoting it—the card is the source of truth for its active rate and minimum payout.
When do commissions become available?
Commissions earned from the 1st–15th are scheduled to release on the 16th; commissions from the 16th–month-end are scheduled for the 1st. Your Earnings page shows the exact release date on each entry.
Why is TDS deducted from a payout?
ONROL deducts tax at source as required by Indian law. Add your valid PAN so the applicable rate can be used, and download your ledger or monthly statement for tax filing.
How long does referral attribution last?
The standard attribution window is 30 days from the tracked click. A valid personal coupon can also connect a checkout to you when a link click is unavailable.
What happens when a learner pays in instalments?
Your commission follows the amount ONROL actually collects. Each instalment can create its own commission entry and release date, visible on the Earnings page.
Does it cost anything to join?
No. Affiliate registration is free. You need a working email address for OTP verification and a mobile number for account and payout communication.
How does my coupon code work?
Create a memorable code on the Coupons page. When a customer enters it at checkout, the configured discount is applied and the eligible sale is attributed to your account.
How can I confirm that a link works?
Open Performance after sharing. You will see tracked clicks, the destination program, channel, device, country, and the click-to-paid funnel as activity arrives.
Where can I promote?
You can promote through WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube, websites, communities, and other honest channels. Tag the channel while building a link so your reporting stays useful.
Can I download reports?
Yes. Performance includes Excel exports for earnings, referred leads, and click activity. Statements provides month-by-month payout and TDS records.
What is the minimum withdrawal?
It varies by program. Check the program card and your Payouts screen for the live threshold rather than publishing a fixed amount.
Can another affiliate join through me?
If a partner-referral campaign is active in your portal, share its dedicated tracked link. Any override commission follows the terms displayed for that campaign.
Who helps if I get stuck?
Use the support option in your portal or email info@onrol.in. Include the page name and a screenshot so the team can resolve it faster.
