AI tools in India — FAQ

How many AI tools are there in India?

Thousands, and rising every week. Almost all global AI tools are accessible in India, plus a growing number of Indian-built tools. Rather than chase the count, focus on assembling a small stack of 5–10 tools that fit your work — this guide lists 60+ of the most useful to choose from.

What are the best AI tools for Indians in 2026?

A solid core stack: a chatbot (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini), a writing aid (Grammarly or Notion AI), an image generator (Midjourney or Adobe Firefly), a coding assistant (GitHub Copilot or Cursor), and an automation tool (Zapier, Make or n8n). Add specialist tools — video, voice, data — as your work demands.

Are AI tools free in India?

Many are. ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Canva and Perplexity all have capable free tiers you can do real work on. Premium plans unlock stronger models, higher limits and pro features, typically ₹1,000–₹2,000/month. You can go a long way before paying anything.

Which AI tool is best for beginners?

ChatGPT or Google Gemini — both are free, need no setup, and are useful for writing, learning, planning and research from day one. The higher-value skill is learning to build with AI, not just use it, which is what ONROL's project-first programs teach.
